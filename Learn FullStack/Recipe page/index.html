Simple Pumpkin Soup Recipe

An easy and delicious recipe for homemade pumpkin soup. this is a great way to enjoy the flavors of fall! filled with warm spices and creamy texture.

Preparation Time

  • Prep Time: 10 minutes
  • Cook Time: 15 minutes
  • Total Time: 25 minutes

Ingredients:

  • 1 can of pumpkin puree
  • 1 cup of chicken broth
  • 1 teaspoon of cinnamon
  • 1/2 teaspoon of ginger
  • 1/4 teaspoon of nutmeg
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions:

  1. In a large pot, combine the pumpkin puree and chicken broth.
  2. Add the cinnamon, ginger, nutmeg, salt, and pepper.
  3. Stir well and bring to a simmer over medium heat.
  4. Cook for 10-15 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  5. Use an immersion blender to puree the soup until smooth.
  6. Serve hot and enjoy!
